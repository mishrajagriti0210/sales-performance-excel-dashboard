# Sales Performance Analysis & Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview
An Excel-based sales analytics project built to evaluate revenue, profitability, regional performance, and product-category contribution. The dashboard is designed to turn transactional sales data into clear business insights for decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales, profit, and order volume.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions.
- Identify the strongest product categories.
- Review monthly sales trends.
- Highlight the difference between revenue growth and profit performance.

## Dataset
The practice dataset contains 100 sales records with fields including Order ID, Date, Region, Product, Category, Quantity, Sales, Cost, Profit, and Profit Margin.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- KPI summaries
- Data analysis and dashboard design

## Key Results
- **Total Sales:** ₹6,853,204.18
- **Total Profit:** ₹2,260,618.25
- **Total Orders:** 100
- **Top sales region:** North
- **Top profit region:** West
- **Top revenue category:** Electronics

## Business Insights
1. Electronics is the largest revenue contributor in the dataset.
2. North leads in sales, while West generates the highest total profit.
3. Regional profit margins differ, so revenue alone does not provide a complete view of performance.
4. Regional pricing, cost structure, and product mix are useful areas for further investigation. The available data does not establish the cause of these differences.
